Research Team

An interdisciplinary team spanning honeybee sensing, controlled-environment agriculture, and field robotics — working across custom PCB & embedded systems design, computer vision, hyperspectral & thermal imaging, wireless sensor networks & IoT, UGV/UAV autonomy & precision navigation, sensor fusion, machine learning & AI, dynamic systems modeling and control, and edge computing for real-world agricultural problems.

Leadership Overview
Founder & Lab Director

Dr. Sulaymon Eshkabilov

Make agriculture and apiculture sustainable using smart sensing, AI, robotics, and automation

Dr. Eshkabilov directs an eight-project research program spanning honeybee biosensing, controlled-environment agriculture, and field robotics. His group designs custom sensor and PCB hardware, and applies computer vision, hyperspectral imaging, sensor fusion, and machine learning to problems in UGV/UAV navigation, precision weeding, hive health monitoring, and hydroponic nutrient control — validated through dynamic system modeling in MATLAB & Simulink and extensive field and greenhouse testing.

"Dr. Eshkabilov's passion for mechanical and control engineering excellence, combined with his dedication to student mentoring, is what makes our research journey both inspiring and transformative."

Professional Experience

Currently — Assistant Professor, NDSU

Dr. Eshkabilov is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering at North Dakota State University (NDSU), Fargo, North Dakota. His research lies at the intersection of agricultural engineering, robotics, computer vision, artificial intelligence, sensing technologies, and precision agriculture.

Dr. Eshkabilov has extensive experience in digital agriculture, machine vision, remote sensing, and autonomous systems for agricultural applications. His research program focuses on developing intelligent sensing and automation technologies to improve agricultural productivity, sustainability, and decision-making. He leads interdisciplinary projects involving agricultural robotics, unmanned aerial systems (UAS), machine learning, deep learning, advanced sensors, and Internet of Things (IoT) technologies for crop and livestock production systems.

Dr. Eshkabilov has secured competitive funding from federal, state, industry, and commodity-group sponsors to support research in precision agriculture and agricultural automation. His work has resulted in numerous peer-reviewed journal publications, conference presentations, and collaborative research initiatives with engineers, computer scientists, animal scientists, agronomists, and industry partners.

At NDSU, he teaches undergraduate and graduate courses in instrumentation & measurement and sensing technologies. He mentors graduate and undergraduate students while preparing the next generation of engineers and researchers to address emerging challenges in agricultural, apicultural, and biological systems.

His current research projects include

Agricultural robotics and autonomous systems Computer vision and deep learning for agriculture Precision farming Precision apiculture Smart sensing and IoT technologies Unmanned aerial systems and remote sensing Agricultural automation and control systems Digital agriculture and decision-support systems AI-assisted crop and honey bee monitoring Intelligent machinery and energy-efficient ag machinery

Research Interests

Dr. Eshkabilov is particularly passionate about the design and implementation of IoT and wireless sensor network technologies, computer vision, and robotics for honey bee and crop monitoring and agricultural operations.
