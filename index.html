// Lab Mindpower & Bios

Core Research Team

An interdisciplinary team spanning honeybee sensing, controlled-environment agriculture, and field robotics — working across custom PCB & embedded systems design, computer vision, hyperspectral & thermal imaging, wireless sensor networks & IoT, UGV/UAV autonomy & precision navigation, sensor fusion, machine learning & AI, dynamic systems modeling and control, and edge computing for real-world agricultural problems.

Leadership Overview
Founder & Lab Director

Dr. Sulaymon Eshkabilov

Visionary Leadership for Sustainable Mechatronics & Control

Dr. Eshkabilov directs an eighteen-project research program spanning honeybee biosensing, controlled-environment agriculture, and field robotics. His group designs custom sensor and PCB hardware, and applies computer vision, hyperspectral imaging, sensor fusion, and machine learning to problems in UGV/UAV navigation, precision weeding, hive health monitoring, and hydroponic nutrient control — validated through dynamic system modeling in MATLAB & Simulink and extensive field and greenhouse testing.

"Dr. Eshkabilov's passion for mechanical and control engineering excellence, combined with his dedication to student mentoring, is what makes our research journey both inspiring and transformative."

Biography

Dr. Eshkabilov earned his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the Academy of Sciences of Uzbekistan, where he focused on applications of genetic algorithms in optimization problems. He received his M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering and Automotive Testing from the Rochester Institute of Technology and the Tashkent Automobile and Road Institute, respectively. He is the author of over 50 research papers published in peer-reviewed journals and conference proceedings, and five books on MATLAB/Simulink applications.

In addition to his research, Dr. Eshkabilov has a strong commitment to teaching and mentoring. He held faculty positions at the Tashkent Automobile and Road Institute and Tashkent Turin Polytechnic University, and was a visiting researcher at Johannes Kepler University, Austria, and Ohio University, USA. He has also served as an external academic expert for the European Commission since 2009.

Outside of his professional work, Dr. Eshkabilov loves to be in wild nature and enjoys mountain hiking.

Professional Experience

Currently — Assistant Professor, NDSU

At North Dakota State University in the Agricultural & Biosystems Engineering Department, his responsibilities are split between research and teaching. Focus areas include machine learning for data processing, image processing, adaptive control, digital hydraulic cylinders, and MEMS data loggers.

Previously, he served as an Assistant Professor at the University of Jamestown (2020–2021) and as a Visiting Scientist/Professor at North Dakota State University (2018–2020), specializing in adaptive control for agricultural machines and image processing for meat and plants. He also held roles at Tashkent-Turin Polytechnic University and the Tashkent Automobile and Road Institute, served as a JESH Scholar at Johannes Kepler University in Austria, and was a Visiting Professor at Ohio University.

Research Interests

He is particularly passionate about the design and implementation of IoT technologies and autonomous all-terrain vehicles in crop monitoring and agricultural operations.
