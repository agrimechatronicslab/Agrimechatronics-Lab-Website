Grant & Hardware Updates

NDSU Awarded $625K NSF-MRI Grant to Acquire State-of-the-Art 3D Laser Scanning Vibrometer

Spanning three colleges and eight university departments, NDSU has secured a $625,244 NSF Major Research Instrumentation (MRI) award to acquire a PSV QTEC 3D Laser Scanning Vibrometer. Led by Assistant Professor Dr. Sulaymon Eshkabilov, this cutting-edge engineering tool allows researchers to capture true 3D non-contact vibratory signals. By measuring frequencies non-invasively, the research team can deeply analyze structural and biological movements—such as determining whether a honey bee hive is calm or under stress.

Kicking off in March 2024, this precision agriculture project combats weed control by dramatically reducing chemical herbicide usage.
